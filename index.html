Beyond our Sun

Exoplanets: planets around other stars

Astronomers have confirmed more than 6,400 planets orbiting other stars. Most are found with the transit method: when a planet crosses in front of its star, the star's light dips by a tiny amount. If the dip repeats on a steady schedule, that is a strong planet candidate - astronomers then run follow-up checks to confirm it, because other things (like a second star passing behind) can mimic the dip. This is a selection of the most interesting ones found so far, with real data from the NASA Exoplanet Archive.

Each dip in the curve is one transit: a planet blocking a little of its star's light.
